# Search history and saved results

Find an earlier search again, keep the ones that matter, and share them.

Every lookup you run appears in your [history](https://www.osintcat.net/dashboard/history), ready to open again.

## How long results are kept

Your plan keeps results for a number of days, shown at the top of your history. Older results are removed after that.

## Keep a search for good

Mark a search to keep it. It is then not removed after the usual days. Your plan allows a number of kept searches; to keep another when you reach it, stop keeping one first.

## Share a search

Share a search from its result or from your history. The link keeps working until you revoke it or delete the search. Deleting a search also stops its share link.
