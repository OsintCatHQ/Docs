# What you can do with OsintCat

The tools you get, from your first search to a whole team's investigations.

## Search

Each module searches for one kind of detail: an email address, a username, a phone number, a domain, an IP address and more. Enter what you know, and the results come back in one place, ready to read, report or export.

## Keep your work together

- **History.** Every search is saved, so you can open it again, keep the important ones and share a result with a link.
- **Researches.** Case files with mind maps, notes and imported lookups, which you can share with the people you work with.
- **Export.** Save results as a file for your report.

## Work your way

- **API.** Run the same lookups from your own tools with an API key, from the Researcher plan or with pay as you go.
- **Companies.** One plan for your team, a seat for each person, company sign-in and invoices with your company details.
- **Pay how you like.** Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, local methods, crypto, balance or gift codes.
- **Your language.** OsintCat works in 12 languages, on your computer and on your phone.

Start for free on the [sign-up page](https://www.osintcat.net/register), no card needed.
