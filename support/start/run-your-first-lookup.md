# Run your first lookup

Pick a module, enter what you know, and read the results.

1. Sign in and open the [dashboard](https://www.osintcat.net/dashboard/home).
2. Choose a module from the menu. Each module searches for one kind of thing, such as an email address, a username or a phone number.
3. Enter what you know and run the search.
4. Read the results. Open a result card to see everything it found.

Every search you run appears in your [history](https://www.osintcat.net/dashboard/history), so you can open it again later.

:::note
**Search responsibly.** Only look up details you are allowed to look up: your own, or as part of work you are authorised to do. Our Terms explain what is allowed.
:::
