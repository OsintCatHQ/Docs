# Change the email on your account

Move your account to a new email address.

1. Open [General](https://www.osintcat.net/account/general) in your account settings.
2. Enter your new email address.
3. We send a code to your current address and another to the new one. Enter both to confirm.

The change applies as soon as both codes are confirmed. From then on, sign-in codes and receipts go to the new address.

:::note
**Company accounts.** If your company manages your account, the new address must be on your company's domain. Ask your company's admin if you need something else.
:::
