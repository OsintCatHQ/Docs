# Ask for your details to be removed

How to send a removal request and what happens next.

You can ask us to remove your personal details from OsintCat search results.

1. Open the [Data Removal page](https://www.osintcat.net/removal).
2. Enter your full legal name, choose the kinds of data you want removed, and enter each detail, such as an email address or phone number.
3. Add documents if you have them; they help us check the request faster.
4. Send the request.

We email you a confirmation with your request ID. Most requests are handled within 15 to 45 days, depending on the privacy law where you live, and we tell you the outcome by email. If we can't accept it as sent, the email says why, and you can send it again with what is missing.
