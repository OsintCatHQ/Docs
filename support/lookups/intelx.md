# IntelX lookups

Search leaks, pastes and darknet sources, then open the full record.

IntelX lets you search across leaks, pastes and darknet sources, and then open the full record behind a result.

## Search

1. Open the IntelX module in your dashboard.
2. Enter an email address, domain, IP address, phone number or keyword.
3. Results appear as they are found. Choose which sources to show; results in other sources are counted, so you know when to widen the search.

## Open a full record

Use **Fetch** on a result, or paste its System ID, to retrieve the full record. Inside a file you can filter for what you need, such as an email domain or a name, instead of reading it all.

## Which plans include it

IntelX is included on Investigator and Max, and with pay as you go. See [Pricing](https://www.osintcat.net/pricing).
