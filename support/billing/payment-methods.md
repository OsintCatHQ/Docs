# Payment methods we accept

Cards, wallets, PayPal, local methods, crypto and balance.

What you see at checkout depends on your country and on what you are buying.

| Method | Notes |
| - | - |
| Card | Visa, Mastercard and Amex, also through Apple Pay and Google Pay. |
| PayPal | Pay from your PayPal balance, bank or linked card. |
| Local methods | Several methods used in your country, shown at checkout when they apply. |
| Crypto | BTC, ETH, USDT, SOL, LTC and other coins. |
| OsintCat balance | Pay from money you added earlier, or with a gift code. |

Some methods can pay once but can't renew a subscription. If you pick one of them for a plan, checkout tells you and you can choose another, or use it to add balance instead.

We don't store your card details. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, and we only receive confirmation that you paid.
