# Change or cancel your plan

Move to a bigger or smaller plan, or stop it from renewing.

## Move to a bigger plan

Choose the plan on [Pricing](https://www.osintcat.net/pricing) or in [Billing](https://www.osintcat.net/billing). It starts right away, and you pay only the difference for the rest of the current period.

## Move to a smaller plan

Choose it the same way. The smaller plan starts when your current period ends, so you keep what you paid for until then.

## Cancel

1. Open [Billing](https://www.osintcat.net/billing).
2. Choose to cancel your subscription and confirm.

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and then does not renew. Until that date you can resume it from the same page.

:::note
**Paid through Telegram?** A plan paid in Telegram is cancelled there, not on our billing page.
:::
