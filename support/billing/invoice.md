# Get an invoice with your company details

Add your company name, address and VAT number, and download invoices.

Every order has an invoice you can download from [Billing](https://www.osintcat.net/billing).

## Add your company details

1. Open [Billing](https://www.osintcat.net/billing), or use the company fields at checkout.
2. Enter your company name, address and VAT number.

From then on, every invoice shows them. If an invoice already went out with the wrong details, you can correct them on that invoice later.
