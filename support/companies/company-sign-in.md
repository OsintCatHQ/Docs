# Sign in with your company

Let members sign in through your company's identity provider.

A company can require its members to sign in through its own identity provider, using OpenID Connect. Set it up in your company's settings with the details from your provider.

When it is required:

- members sign in with their company account,
- API keys made without company sign-in stop working, and the member creates a new one,
- the email address on a member's account must be on the company's domain.
