# Create a company

Set up a company, invite your team, and keep your personal account.

A company lets a team search together under one plan. Searches made inside the company belong to it and are visible to its admins. Your personal account and its searches stay yours.

1. Open [Create a company](https://www.osintcat.net/org/new) and give it a name.
2. Choose a plan and the seats you need.
3. Invite people by email, up to 50 at once.

After that, switch between your personal account and the company in the account menu.
