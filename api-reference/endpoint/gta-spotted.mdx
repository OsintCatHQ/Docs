title: 'GTA Online Sightings'
api: 'GET https://www.osintcat.net/api/gta/spotted'
description: 'Where and when GTA Online players were seen'
---

Returns how often and when GTA Online players were seen in sessions, by display name or Rockstar ID.

## Parameters

<ParamField query="query" type="string" required>
  A player name or RID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="type" type="string" default="name">
  `name` (default) or `rid`.
</ParamField>

## Headers

<ParamField header="X-API-KEY" type="string" required>
  Your API key.
</ParamField>

## Usage

Counts as one lookup against your plan's daily allowance (Max: unlimited). When the allowance is used up, the lookup can continue at a per-lookup price charged to your balance (the module's page in the dashboard shows the price); a lookup that finds nothing is not charged.
